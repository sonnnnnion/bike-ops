Role & Boundaries

Where bikes may go, who may ride, and what each role is responsible for.

Operating boundaries

Bike Manager

Keeps the fleet serviceable: maintains the bikes, organizes supplies, monitors readiness, and trains members.

Bike Response

Members trained by the Bike Manager to ride. A Bike Responder is responsible for the bike while they have it — checking it before a ride, riding it within bounds, locking and returning it, and reporting anything wrong.
